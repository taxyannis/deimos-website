## 1. Final positioning

Deimos Group is a boutique, independent advisory platform for complex private-market transactions — capital formation, transaction structuring, M&A and joint venture advisory, special situations, and execution support across real assets, infrastructure, energy, natural resources, hospitality, sports, media, lifestyle assets, sovereign-linked development, and public-private/concession opportunities.

Deimos enters where opportunities are valuable but incomplete, under-structured, cross-border, capital-intensive, politically or commercially complex, or not yet bankable for conventional institutional capital. Its role is to structure, sharpen, diligence, position, finance, and advance transactions into executable mandates.

**Register vs. identity — the one resolved conflict.** The site should read in the *register* of private investment banking (Rothschild & Co, PJT Partners, Lazard, Post Oak Group, Houlihan Lokey calibration — tone, rigor, hierarchy, restraint) without asserting that Deimos *is* a regulated investment bank, broker-dealer, or licensed financial institution. Where the backbone brief uses "independent private investment banking … platform" as a tone descriptor (hero copy, SEO metadata, section framing), that usage is fine. It becomes a problem only if rendered as a literal corporate/regulatory identity claim ("Deimos is an investment bank"). `PRODUCT.md`'s own language — "boutique advisory firm" — is the controlling self-description; "investment banking" is the *register*, not the *entity type*.

**Confirmed, not a gap.** Deimos does hold a real license in a specific jurisdiction, but this is a deliberate discretion decision, not an oversight: the license is not named anywhere in public site copy — not in the headline, not in the Firm page, not in footer fine print. It is disclosed only in direct commercial dialogue. The register-only framing above is therefore a chosen posture, not a workaround for a missing credential.

Deimos is explicitly **not**: a broker, finder, passive introducer, listing platform, venture studio, fund, accelerator, AI company, real estate agency, generic consultancy, or investor-matching platform.

**Primary website message:** Deimos advises on complex private-market transactions where capital access, transaction structure, stakeholder alignment, and execution discipline determine whether an opportunity can move from interest to mandate. Deimos is not a passive introducer — it works across defined advisory workstreams to sharpen the transaction, prepare the capital narrative, identify execution gaps, align counterparties, and support the process required to advance complex opportunities.

## 2. Audience

Institutional capital allocators, sponsors, and deal originators operating in complex private-market transactions — real assets, infrastructure, energy, sports, hospitality, PPP/concession, and special-situation opportunities.

Specifically: fund principals, deal sponsors, family offices, institutional investors, asset owners, developers, operators, public-sector stakeholders, concession holders, and government-linked counterparties. These are sophisticated, credential-literate readers judging institutional seriousness within seconds, evaluating whether Deimos has the standing to be trusted with early-stage, under-structured, high-stakes deals. They arrive skeptical of generic advisory marketing.

---

## 3. Website objective

Rebuild the site into a polished, institutional, capital-markets-facing platform. Success is a qualified visitor — a sponsor, deal originator, or allocator — initiating contact to discuss a mandate or transaction. Every page funnels toward that one outcome; no scattered CTAs, no diluted asks.

The site must read as an institution that has been doing this for decades — never as a startup landing page, SaaS product, pitch deck, AI-agency page, capital-introduction site, or generic advisory brochure.

## 18. Motion system

Choreographed but understated. Motion is evidence of scale, jurisdictional reach, or transaction complexity — earned only in those moments (hero slider, map reveals); everywhere else, restrained and functional.

**Appropriate:** smooth fade-ins, subtle upward text reveals, controlled section transitions, refined hover states, understated line animations, smooth tab transitions, gentle video crossfades, elegant progress indicators, map hover highlights, restrained parallax only where it improves depth.

**Avoid:** bouncing elements, excessive scroll-jacking, loud cursor effects, spinning icons, overdone 3D, aggressive zooms, fast slide changes, heavy particle effects, animated clutter, gaming/crypto-style transitions.

`prefers-reduced-motion` alternative required for every animation, consistent with the institutional, no-surprises tone (PRODUCT.md accessibility baseline).
