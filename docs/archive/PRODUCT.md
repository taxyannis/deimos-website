# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Institutional capital allocators, sponsors, and deal originators operating in complex private-market transactions — real assets, infrastructure, energy, sports, hospitality, PPP/concession, and special-situation opportunities. These are sophisticated, credential-literate readers (fund principals, deal sponsors, family offices, institutional investors) evaluating whether Deimos has the standing and track record to be trusted with early-stage, under-structured, high-stakes deals. They arrive skeptical of generic advisory marketing and are judging institutional seriousness within seconds.

## Product Purpose

Deimos is a boutique advisory firm specializing in private-market transaction structuring, capital formation, strategic investor access, and execution support. Its specialty is taking early-stage or under-structured opportunities — deals that aren't yet investable, bankable, or mandate-ready — and converting them into something institutional capital can act on. Success is a qualified visitor (a sponsor, deal originator, or allocator) initiating contact to discuss a mandate or transaction.

## Brand Personality

Confident, authoritative, precise. Institutional seriousness in the register of Houlihan Lokey or Lazard, but with more cinematic private-market presentation: restrained typography, high-contrast editorial layouts, confident whitespace, a premium transaction-advisory tone, controlled (not decorative) motion, metric-led hero sections, and subtle aerial/video-backed sections that evoke the physical scale of the assets involved (infrastructure, energy, real assets).

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS / AI-startup aesthetics: cream or off-white gradient backgrounds, gradient-clip text, the hero-metric-card template, uniform icon-grid feature sections, playful tech-brand tone, startup dashboard UI patterns. Nothing about this site should read as a funded seed-stage product — it should read as an institution that has been doing this for decades.

## Design Principles

1. **Institutional gravity over startup polish** — every visual choice should read as a serious, credentialed advisory firm, not a SaaS product or pitch deck.
2. **Precision as a value proposition** — the site's own restraint and structural discipline should demonstrate the same rigor Deimos brings to structuring complex transactions. Sloppy hierarchy undercuts the pitch.
3. **Show scale and scope, not features** — real assets, infrastructure, energy, sports, hospitality, and PPP are physical, cinematic domains. Lead with imagery and metrics that evoke scale and consequence, not icon grids or feature lists.
4. **Confidence through whitespace and typography, not decoration** — authority is carried by restraint, contrast, and hierarchy, not by ornament, gradients, or motion for its own sake.
5. **One clear path to contact** — every page should funnel toward a mandate/transaction conversation; avoid scattering CTAs or diluting the ask.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal requirement specified by the client. Default to solid WCAG AA practice as a baseline: 4.5:1 body text contrast, full keyboard navigation, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation — consistent with the institutional, no-surprises tone the brand calls for.
